Topic 1: Problem Statement & Market Context

The Broken Conventional Agricultural Supply Chain

Why smallholder farmers remain in poverty while consumers pay exorbitant prices for stale produce

01
Intermediary Exploitation
  • 4 to 6 Middlemen Tiers: Village brokers, APMC commission agents, secondary wholesalers, distributors, retail vendors.
  • Severe Margin Theft: Farmer receives barely 25%–30% of the consumer's rupee.
  • Hidden Deductions: APMC weighment cuts, bag levies, and delayed payments trap farmers in debt cycles.
02
Severe Post-Harvest Spoilage
  • 35% to 40% Wastage: Perishables rot over 5 to 7 days of uncoordinated transit and multi-stage loading.
  • ₹92,000+ Crore Loss: National food supply loss every year due to lack of direct pre-booked supply lines.
  • Distress Selling: Bumper harvests cause localized gluts where farmers dump crops at ₹1–2/kg.
03
Advisory & Pathology Void
  • Blind Sowing: Farmers plant crops without statistical demand indicators, causing cyclical market crashes.
  • Unchecked Crop Pathologies: Fungal and bacterial infections devastate acres without timely agronomist advice.
  • Scarcity of Officers: Over 1,200 farm holdings per 1 extension officer creates an access crisis.

Topic 2: Technical Approach — Architecture

System Architecture & Engineering Implementation

A high-performance, modular full-stack web architecture designed for low latency and rural accessibility

Architecture Highlights
  • Frontend Presentation Tier: React 18 SPA built with Vite for sub-second page loads. Styled with Tailwind CSS for 100% mobile-responsive touch screens.
  • Backend Microservices API: Node.js & Express RESTful API gateway handling user sessions, order matching, and file uploads.
  • Security & Authentication: Stateless JWT tokens with strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) protecting endpoints.
  • Pathology Image Stream: Multer multipart file stream with secure disk hashing and file-type validation.
  • Persistence Tier: SQLite / sql.js providing transactional ACID consistency with zero server maintenance overhead.
  • Cloud CI/CD: Automatic continuous deployment pipeline to Render from GitHub main repository.

6 Distinct Role-Based Portals & Operational Synergy

Granular role isolation ensures total accountability across the agricultural supply chain

1. Farmer Portal
List fresh produce, review statistical ROI advisory per crop, upload diseased crop photos for triage, track 75% balance payments.
2. Retail Consumer Portal
Browse seasonal harvest, choose Hub Pickup (min ₹100) or Home Delivery (min ₹300), pay 25% commitment escrow upfront.
3. Bulk Buyer Portal
Institutional orders for canteens, hostels, and hotels with tiered volume discounts and consolidated dispatch logistics.
4. Regional Hub Coordinator
Manage village aggregation hub, weigh & grade incoming crops, verify consumer pickup, and collect remaining 75% payment.
5. Agricultural Adviser
Tele-pathology dashboard: inspect uploaded high-res leaf photos, diagnose fungal/pest infections, and send structured advice.
6. Admin Super-Portal
Platform governance: mandatory KYC approval gate for Farmers, Coordinators, and Advisers before they can transact or advise.
